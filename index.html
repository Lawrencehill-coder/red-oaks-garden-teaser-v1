The Opportunity

Allion Fund 1, LP is deploying $5,000,000 in debt capital to complete construction and bring the remaining homes in the Red Oak Gardens subdivision to market. Horizontal infrastructure and the model home are complete. Three additional homes are now under active construction. Five lots have already sold to another builder, reducing the fund's remaining exposure within the subdivision. Existing senior capital is in place and verified against official bank payoff statements.

Fund Structure

Capital is raised through Allion Fund 1, LP, a Regulation D Rule 506(c) real estate debt fund. Investors hold a debt position targeting a preferred return of 12% to 15%, senior to project-level equity. The Red Oak Gardens development is one of the assets financed through the fund. Investment is limited to verified accredited investors as required under Rule 506(c). Final terms are set through the fund's offering documents and subscription agreement, not through this summary.
