The Opportunity

Pedison USA is arranging $5,000,000 in capital to complete construction and bring to market the final 18 homes in the Red Oak Gardens subdivision in Red Oak, Texas. This is not a ground-up start. Infrastructure work and multiple homes are already underway, with existing senior capital in place and verified against official bank payoff statements.

This raise is structured as a joint venture position alongside Pedison and the development team, not a fixed-terms subscription.
