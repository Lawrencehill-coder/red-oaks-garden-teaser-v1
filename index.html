Executive Financing Summary

Proposed senior secured debt facility: $6,710,076. Red Oak Gardens is a residential development in Red Oak, Ellis County, Texas. The proposed facility would refinance eligible existing project debt and finance construction of approximately 18 remaining single-family residences, subject to updated borrower authorization, current project information, and lender credit approval.

Three existing homes at 117, 122, and 130 Yvonne Street were financed through PlainsCapital Bank. Their current inventory, sales and lien status must be confirmed. The borrower previously advised it was discontinuing the Wallis Bank process; this presentation does not imply that Wallis Bank is considering or approving a loan.

Credit Considerations and Repayment

  • Primary repayment: Net proceeds from individual home sales, applied through lender-approved lot or home release provisions.
  • Collateral: First-priority security over eligible project real estate, subject to appraisal, title and lien verification.
  • Construction controls: Approved budget, draw inspections, lien waivers, insurance and customary retainage provisions.
  • Key underwriting risks: Unsold completed homes, market absorption, cost overruns, sponsor liquidity and timely completion.
  • Financial sensitivity: Lender should test slower sales, reduced prices, increased costs and delayed closings.
